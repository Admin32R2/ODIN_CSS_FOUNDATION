Nilagang Baka Recipe


Description

Nilagang Baka is the quintessential Filipino comfort beef soup. Tender chunks of beef shank and marrow bones are simmered slowly with whole peppercorns and sweet onions until the broth is deeply rich, savory, and soothing.

Generously packed with wholesome vegetables including sweet yellow corn cobs, hearty potatoes, green beans, and crisp cabbage, it is the ultimate remedy for rainy days and family dinners, always eaten with hot rice and fish sauce (patis) with calamansi.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank or brisket, cut into large chunks
  • 8 cups water (or beef stock)
  • 1 large white onion, quartered
  • 1 tablespoon whole black peppercorns
  • 2 pieces sweet corn on the cob, cut into rounds
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 1 small head green cabbage, sliced into wedges
  • 1 bundle bok choy (pechay), root ends trimmed
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce (patis), plus more for dipping
  • 1 bunch green onions, cut into 2-inch lengths
  • Calamansi or lemon wedges for dipping

Steps

  1. Place the beef chunks in a large stockpot and cover with cold water. Bring to a rapid boil for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse the meat to remove excess impurities.
  2. Return the clean beef to the pot, add 8 cups of fresh water, quartered onion, and whole black peppercorns.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and gently simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours until the beef is melt-in-your-mouth tender.
  4. Add the cut corn rounds and potato quarters. Simmer for 10 to 12 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender.
  5. Season the broth with fish sauce (patis) to taste.
  6. Turn off the heat and add the cabbage wedges, bok choy, and green onions. Cover and let the residual heat gently steam the greens for 3 minutes so they stay vibrant.
  7. Ladle the steaming soup, beef, and vegetables into large bowls and serve immediately with hot rice and patis-calamansi dip.

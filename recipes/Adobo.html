Adobo Recipe


Description

Chicken Adobo is a classic Filipino dish made by simmering chicken in a flavorful mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. It is known for its savory, tangy, and slightly sweet flavor.

The chicken is slowly cooked until tender and absorbs the rich adobo sauce. It is commonly served with steamed white rice, making it a comforting and popular Filipino meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • Steamed rice, for serving

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for about 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan and brown the chicken pieces.
  3. Add the remaining marinade, vinegar, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  4. Bring the mixture to a boil without stirring.
  5. Lower the heat and simmer until the chicken becomes tender.
  6. Add the brown sugar and continue cooking until the sauce thickens slightly.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.

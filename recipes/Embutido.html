Embutido Recipe


Description

Embutido is a traditional Filipino-style steamed meatloaf rolled in foil, often prepared for Christmas (Noche Buena), birthdays, and family fiestas. Made with seasoned ground pork, sweet raisins, minced bell peppers, cheddar cheese, and hard-boiled eggs hidden in the center, it delivers a wonderful contrast of sweet and savory notes.

It can be enjoyed warm straight from the steamer or chilled and fried until the exterior gets a golden, caramelized crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ground pork
  • 1/2 cup sweet pickle relish
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup finely minced red and green bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup grated cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup bread crumbs
  • 2 raw eggs, beaten
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, quartered lengthwise
  • 2 Filipino hotdogs or vienna sausages, sliced lengthwise
  • 1/4 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Aluminum foil for wrapping

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground pork, pickle relish, raisins, bell peppers, cheese, bread crumbs, beaten raw eggs, tomato sauce, salt, and pepper. Mix until evenly blended.
  2. Cut aluminum foil into 10x12-inch sheets.
  3. Spread about 1 to 1.5 cups of the meat mixture flat onto the center of each foil sheet.
  4. Arrange slices of hard-boiled eggs and hotdog strips down the middle of the meat layer.
  5. Carefully roll the foil over the meat into a tight cylinder, twisting and tucking the ends securely to prevent steam from entering.
  6. Place the wrapped rolls into a steamer and steam over medium-low heat for 50 to 60 minutes.
  7. Allow the embutido to cool completely before slicing into rounds. Serve as-is or pan-fry lightly in oil.

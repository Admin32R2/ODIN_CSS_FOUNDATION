Inihaw na Liempo Recipe


Description

Inihaw na Liempo is Filipino grilled pork belly at its finest. Thick-cut slabs of succulent pork belly are marinated in soy sauce, calamansi juice, garlic, brown sugar, and banana ketchup before being charred to perfection over glowing wood coals.

Crispy edges, meltingly tender fat, and caramelized sweet-tangy marinade make this an unbeatable staple at beach trips, picnics, and casual family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly (liempo), sliced about 1/2-inch thick
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup calamansi juice or lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup banana ketchup (or tomato ketchup)
  • 1 head garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil (for basting)
  • Spiced cane vinegar (sukang Iloko or sinamak) with chili and onion for dipping

Steps

  1. In a shallow container, mix together the soy sauce, calamansi juice, banana ketchup, garlic, brown sugar, and black pepper.
  2. Submerge the sliced pork belly in the marinade. Refrigerate and marinate for at least 4 hours, or overnight for best flavor penetration.
  3. Before grilling, strain the marinade into a small saucepan and simmer with 1 tablespoon oil for 5 minutes to create a basting glaze.
  4. Prepare your grill with hot coals to medium-high heat.
  5. Grill the pork belly for 5 to 7 minutes per side, brushing generously with the basting sauce during the last few minutes.
  6. Once beautifully caramelized and cooked through with slight charred edges, remove from heat.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes, chop into bite-sized strips, and serve with spiced vinegar and warm rice.

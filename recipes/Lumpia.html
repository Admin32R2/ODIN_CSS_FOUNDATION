Lumpia (Lumpiang Shanghai) Recipe


Description

Lumpiang Shanghai is arguably the most essential centerpiece of any Filipino gathering or celebration. These crispy, golden-brown fried spring rolls are filled with a savory mixture of minced pork, carrots, onions, garlic, and seasoning wrapped snugly in thin spring roll pastry.

Crispy on the outside and tender and juicy on the inside, they are typically served cut into bite-sized pieces alongside sweet and sour sauce or spicy banana ketchup.

Ingredients

  • 500g ground pork
  • 1 medium carrot, finely minced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onions or spring onions
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 30 to 40 thin lumpia (spring roll) wrappers
  • Cooking oil for deep frying
  • Sweet and sour sauce for dipping

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly mix the ground pork, minced carrot, onion, garlic, spring onions, egg, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Lay a lumpia wrapper flat on a clean cutting board in a diamond orientation.
  3. Place about 1 to 2 tablespoons of filling in a thin log across the lower center of the wrapper.
  4. Fold the bottom corner up over the filling, tuck in the left and right sides tightly, and roll forward snugly.
  5. Dab the top corner with a little water to seal the wrapper closed. Repeat for the remaining mixture.
  6. Heat oil in a deep pan or skillet over medium heat (around 350°F / 175°C).
  7. Fry the lumpia in batches for 4 to 6 minutes, turning occasionally until golden brown and crispy.
  8. Drain on a wire rack or paper towels, slice in half diagonally, and serve with sweet and sour sauce.

Kare-Kare Recipe


Description

Kare-Kare is a luxurious Filipino stew celebrated for its rich, thick peanut sauce colored golden with annatto. Traditionally prepared with tender oxtail, beef shank, and beef tripe, it is accompanied by an assortment of fresh vegetables such as string beans, eggplant, and bok choy.

The stew itself is subtly savory and nutty, designed specifically to be paired with pungent, salty sautéed shrimp paste (bagoong alamang), creating a harmony of contrasting flavors.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg oxtail or beef shank, cut into chunks
  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter (or ground roasted peanuts)
  • 1/2 cup toasted glutinous rice flour (for thickening)
  • 2 tablespoons annatto powder (dissolved in 1/4 cup warm water)
  • 1 bundle sitaw (long beans), cut into 2-inch segments
  • 2 medium eggplants, sliced diagonally
  • 1 bundle pechay or bok choy, ends trimmed
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 to 8 cups water or beef broth
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Sautéed shrimp paste (bagoong alamang), for serving

Steps

  1. Place the oxtail in a large pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil, skim off scum, and simmer on low heat for 2 to 3 hours until fork-tender. Reserve 4 cups of broth.
  2. In a separate pot, blanch the string beans, eggplant, and bok choy until just tender. Drain and set aside to keep vegetables vibrant.
  3. In a large pot, heat oil over medium heat. Sauté garlic and onion until soft and fragrant.
  4. Add the cooked oxtail and 4 cups of reserved beef broth. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Whisk in the peanut butter and the dissolved annatto mixture until smoothly incorporated.
  6. Mix toasted rice flour with 1/2 cup water until smooth, then slowly pour into the simmering stew while stirring continuously until thick and velvety.
  7. Simmer for another 5 minutes, then arrange the blanched vegetables on top or gently fold them in.
  8. Serve hot with steamed white rice and a generous side of sautéed bagoong alamang.

Igado Recipe


Description

Igado is a classic spiced stew hailing from the Ilocos region in northern Philippines. The name comes from "higado," the Spanish word for liver. It features strips of pork tenderloin and pork liver cooked in a tangy sauce of vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and bay leaves, complemented by green peas and bell peppers.

Hearty and packed with iron and deep savory flavor, Igado balances the natural richness of pork liver with the bright acidity of cane vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 500g pork tenderloin, cut into thin strips
  • 250g pork liver, cut into thin strips
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup cane vinegar
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium red onion, chopped
  • 1 medium red bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup water or pork broth
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Marinate the pork tenderloin and pork liver separately in half of the soy sauce and minced garlic for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion and remaining garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the marinated pork tenderloin strips and cook until browned on all sides.
  4. Pour in the cane vinegar, bay leaves, and water. Bring to a boil without stirring, then simmer for 15 minutes until pork is tender.
  5. Add the marinated liver and cook for 5 to 7 minutes (avoid overcooking the liver so it stays tender).
  6. Stir in the red bell peppers and green peas, cooking for another 3 minutes until tender-crisp.
  7. Season with salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Isaw (Grilled Chicken Intestines) Recipe


Description

Isaw is one of the ultimate crown jewels of Philippine street food culture. Typically made from thoroughly cleaned chicken intestines coiled like a wave onto bamboo skewers, it is first boiled until tender, marinated in a flavorful barbecue mixture, and then grilled over flaming charcoal.

Crisped around the edges with a tender chew, isaw is traditionally dipped into spiced vinegar loaded with onions, garlic, and bird's eye chilies (sili labuyo), or sweet-savory street food sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken intestines, thoroughly cleaned inside and out
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup banana ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Bamboo skewers, soaked in water for 30 minutes
  • Spiced cane vinegar with diced onions and chili for dipping

Steps

  1. Place the cleaned chicken intestines in a pot with water, vinegar, crushed garlic, and ginger slices. Bring to a boil and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes to sanitize and tenderize. Drain and cool.
  2. In a bowl, mix soy sauce, banana ketchup, brown sugar, black pepper, and cooking oil for the marinade and basting glaze.
  3. Thread the cooled intestines onto soaked bamboo skewers in an undulating zigzag pattern.
  4. Coat the skewers with half of the marinade and let sit for 15 minutes.
  5. Preheat charcoal grill to medium-high heat.
  6. Grill skewers for 3 to 5 minutes per side, constantly brushing with the remaining basting sauce until lightly charred and smoky.
  7. Serve immediately right off the grill with a jar of spicy dipping vinegar.

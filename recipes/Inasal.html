Inasal (Chicken Inasal) Recipe


Description

Chicken Inasal is a renowned Visayan grilled chicken dish originating from Bacolod City. The chicken is marinated in a vibrant blend of calamansi, lemongrass, ginger, garlic, and coconut vinegar, then basted with an aromatic annatto (atsuete) oil while grilling over hot charcoal.

Served traditionally on a banana leaf alongside garlic fried rice and chicken oil (inasal oil), it offers a distinctive smoky, citrusy, and savory flavor profile celebrated all across the Philippines.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken leg quarters or drumsticks
  • 1/4 cup coconut vinegar or cane vinegar
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice (or lime juice)
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, minced
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger, grated
  • 6 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons annatto oil (for basting)
  • 2 tablespoons melted margarine or butter (for basting)

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine vinegar, calamansi juice, lemongrass, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and massage the marinade thoroughly into the meat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  3. In a small bowl, mix the annatto oil and melted margarine to prepare the basting sauce.
  4. Preheat your charcoal grill or indoor grill pan to medium-high heat.
  5. Grill the marinated chicken for 15 to 20 minutes per side, turning occasionally and brushing liberally with the basting oil until fully cooked and lightly charred.
  6. Transfer to a platter and serve hot with steamed garlic rice, sinamak (spiced vinegar), and calamansi toyomansi dip.
